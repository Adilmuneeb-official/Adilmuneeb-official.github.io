Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short

NovaPDF does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your documents are processed entirely on your device and are never sent anywhere.

The only times NovaPDF uses the internet are the three cases listed in Section 3 — and none of them involves your documents or personal information.

01

We collect nothing

NovaPDF does not collect, store, or share personal data or documents. Specifically:

  • No personal data — we do not collect your name, email address, device identifiers, or any other information about you.
  • No documents — the files you open in NovaPDF are never collected or uploaded.
  • No analytics — the app contains no analytics or usage-reporting tools.
  • No advertising — the app contains no ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking — nothing in the app follows your activity, in the app or anywhere else.
  • No accounts — there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to sell, rent, or share with third parties.

02

Your documents stay on your device

All document processing in NovaPDF happens entirely on your device. This includes editing, merging, splitting, converting, protecting, unlocking, and OCR (text recognition).

Your documents are never transmitted anywhere — not to us, and not to any other server. The files you create are saved on your device, in the location you choose, and remain under your control. Deleting the app or the files removes them; we hold no copies.

03

When NovaPDF uses the internet

NovaPDF works offline. There are only three cases where the app connects to the internet, and each one happens only when you choose it:

  • OCR language downloads

    English text recognition is built in. If you choose to add another OCR language, the app downloads that language file — and only that language file — from the public Tesseract repository on GitHub. No document or personal data is sent with the download.

  • HTML to PDF

    If you use the HTML-to-PDF tool and enter a web address, the app fetches that page so it can convert it to a PDF on your device. This only happens for the address you enter, when you ask for it.

  • Purchases

    If you choose to buy NovaPDF Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play, as described in Section 4.

Outside of these three cases, NovaPDF makes no network connections.

04

Purchases and Google Play

Purchases of NovaPDF Pro are handled entirely by Google Play. Your payment is processed by Google using the payment method on your Google account.

We never see, receive, or store your payment card details or other payment information. Google Play confirms your completed purchase to the app so that Pro features can be unlocked on your device. Refunds and purchase history are managed through Google Play, under Google's own policies.

05

Permissions

NovaPDF asks only for what it needs to work:

  • Internet — used only for the three cases in Section 3: OCR language downloads, the HTML-to-PDF fetch you request, and Google Play purchases.
  • Files you pick — NovaPDF can only access files you select yourself through the Android system file picker, plus the files the app creates for you.

NovaPDF does not request access to any of the following:

  • No contacts
  • No location
  • No camera
  • No microphone
06

Children

NovaPDF is a general-purpose document tool and is not directed at children. As explained in Section 1, the app collects no data from anyone — including children.

07

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date at the top. Please check this page from time to time to stay informed. Continued use of NovaPDF after a change means you accept the updated policy.
